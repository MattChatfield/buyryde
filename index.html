About Ryde

Ryde is one of the Isle of Wight's strongest commercial centres, combining a busy town-centre trade with a steady stream of visitors arriving by ferry, fast catamaran and hovercraft. Union Street sits in the heart of that activity, giving this former financial services premises excellent visibility and footfall in a location that already supports independent retail, hospitality and professional services.

The property has a proud local history, having previously operated as Timothy Whites and then TSB Bank, before serving as a financial services business for more than twenty years. Its flexible layout, lower ground accommodation and rear yard make it particularly attractive for a wide range of Class E uses, from retail and dining to professional or lifestyle-led occupiers.

With heritage-led regeneration and investment in the town centre underway, Ryde is positioning itself as an increasingly desirable place to trade, invest and grow. Over the next ten years, Ryde will benefit from Government funding of £10 million, with the Ryde Neighbourhood Board established to ensure those funds are directed wisely to enhance the town and the wider community.
